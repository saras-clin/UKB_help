# UK Biobank Clinical Data Guide

A practical guide for new UK Biobank users at Steno Diabetes Center Aarhus working with clinical records on the Research Analysis Platform (RAP).

This site covers what comes after the initial [ukbAid](https://steno-aarhus.github.io/ukbAid/) setup: how to extract, clean, and work with GP diagnoses, hospital episodes, and medication prescriptions.

**[View the guide](https://saras-clin.github.io/UKB_help/)**
